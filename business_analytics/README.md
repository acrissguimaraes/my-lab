# Google Analytics 4 & Looker Studio – E-commerce Performance Dashboard

An interactive and data-driven dashboard built to analyze the performance, user behavior, and revenue of the **Google Merchandise Store** using real-world data from the Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Demo Account.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project transforms raw web analytics data into actionable business intelligence. The goal is to understand how users interact with the store, identify top-performing acquisition channels, evaluate mobile vs. desktop behavior, and pinpoint high-value temporal trends to optimize marketing ROI.

## 📊 Key Features & Metrics Analyzed
- **Overview Performance:** Monitoring of *Total Users*, *Sessions*, *Views*, and *Average Engagement Time*.
- **Traffic Acquisition:** Analysis of the *Default Channel Grouping* to find out where users come from.
- **Content & Products:** Identification of top-selling items using *Item Name* and *Page Path*.
- **Device & Demographics:** Segmentation of users by *Device Category*, *Age*, and *Gender*.
- **Temporal Analysis:** Visualizing traffic spikes by *Hour* and *Day of the week* over the last year.
- **E-commerce Revenue:** Tracking *Total Revenue* and *Items Purchased*.

---

## 💡 Business Insights & Recommendations

### 1. Mobile Optimization & Targeted Ads
* **Insight:** Over **55% of traffic** comes from mobile/tablet devices, and the **18-34 age group** dominates.
* **Action:** Streamline the mobile checkout process to reduce friction. Launch social media ad campaigns directly optimized for mobile users.

### 2. Time-Triggered Marketing Strategy
* **Insight:** Traffic peaks at **10:00 AM on weekdays** and drops significantly during weekends.
* **Action:** Schedule email newsletters and Paid Search (PPC) ads to launch on **Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays morning** to maximize CTR and conversion rates.

### 3. Boosting Average Order Value (AOV)
* **Insight:** Top-selling items are low-cost goods (pens, stickers), while Total Revenue stands at **$2.68M**.
* **Action:** Implement **Cross-Selling** tactics at checkout (e.g., *"Add a Google Pen for $1 when buying a sweatshirt"*) and create bundled **"Office Packs"** to increase cart value.
